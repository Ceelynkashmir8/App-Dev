## 🩸 ABOUT THE MOVIE

The Conjuring is a supernatural horror film inspired by the paranormal investigations of Ed and Lorraine Warren.

The story follows the Perron family, who move into an old farmhouse in Rhode Island. After experiencing terrifying and unexplained events, they seek help from the Warrens, who investigate the disturbing presence inside their home.

As the investigation continues, they discover that the house holds a dark and dangerous history.
